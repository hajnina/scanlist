### Lead note 2026-11-17 (A4 review deferred nits — RN scan sources, polish card scope)

- `feedSource.ts:102-108`: `stop()` sets `busy=false` while a process is in flight — a restart can overlap two ML Kit calls and deliver a stale result to the NEW listener (`:82` reads mutable `onSignal`). Needs an epoch/generation counter or busy-wait.

- QR is currently unreachable in-app (ML Kit EAN/UPC-only in `native.ts`); the dead fallback + 0.85 claim stay documented as such. If product later wants QR, enable `QR_CODE` in `MLKIT_PRODUCT_FORMATS` and restore the README step.
