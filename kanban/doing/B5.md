### Mandatory decision 1 — sensor-oriented frames are rotated BEFORE embedding ✅
B4 verified the IMAGE JPEG is sensor-sideways (`rotationDegrees` went to ML Kit only). Additive, disclosed
change: `ScanSignal.rotationDegrees: Int = 0`, `ScanFrameCore.onFrame(nowMs, rotationDegrees = 0) { … }` and
`CameraScanAnalyzer` passes `image.imageInfo.rotationDegrees`. `ScanSession.embedImage` computes
`SensorRotation.uprightRotation(...)` (fold to 0/90/180/270 clockwise; reject non-multiples of 90), runs
`ImageRotator.rotateClockwise` and only then calls `embedder.image(upright)`. A frame that cannot be rotated
is **not embedded at all** (a sideways query scores against the wrong geometry); text/barcode keep working.
`JpegRotation` is the Android `Bitmap`/`Matrix` half; the decision is JVM-tested:
`SensorRotationTest` (pass-through, −90/450/360 folding, non-quarter rejection, 90/270 size swap) and
`ScanSessionTest` (rotator called with 90/270, embedder sees the rotated bytes, 0° skips the bitmap job,
rotation failure → zero image embeds). `uprightRotation` is exactly CameraX's "clockwise turn to upright".

### Mandatory decision 2 — pool order: barcode first, extra OCR lines dropped ✅
`SignalPool.textQueries()` is the single owner of the 16-query window: OFF barcode text is **always slot 1**
when present, OCR follows in arrival order, the merged list is capped at 16 (= barcode + at most 15 OCR), and
lines equal to the barcode text or the raw digits are never embedded (RANK.md §8). The rank package is
untouched — its `take(16)` can now only ever drop OCR lines. The risk case is a pinned test: 16 OCR lines
first, then a barcode whose OFF answer arrives last → `pendingTextQueries == [Nutella, line 1…line 15]`, and
`line 16` is the one dropped. This also covers the lead's "cap OCR at 15 when a barcode is present" (the cap
is on the merged list, so barcode-last is safe too). TTLs: OCR 3 s, image 2.5 s, barcode + its name 30 s.
No rank-round change is needed for this card; A5's RN twin still needs the same rule.

### B4 deferred nits — applied / verified
- `CameraScanAnalyzer`: `InputImage.fromMediaImage` + `core.onFrame`/`onSignal` moved inside `try`; a per-frame
  `pending` close plus an `inFlight` counter means `close()` defers closing the ML Kit readers until the last
  frame's callbacks finish, and `analyze` rejects post-close frames (closing the `ImageProxy` immediately).
- `CameraScanPreview`: `rememberUpdatedState(onSignal)` — the `DisposableEffect` no longer captures a stale
  lambda and is not keyed on it (no camera re-bind); `LifecycleResumeEffect` re-checks CAMERA on every resume,
  so returning from Settings starts the preview instead of leaving the dead permission box.
- `AndroidJpegEncoder`: honours `compressToJpeg`'s boolean (false → null, not broken JPEG bytes).
- `OcrLineBatcher.batch(nowMs = 0L)`: **verified, not changed**. Production always passes `nowMs`
  (`ScanFrameCore`), and the default is only the fake-frame tests' convenience; removing it would churn 3 B4
  source tests for no runtime gain. Flagged here instead of editing B4's tests.
- Single-flight/none: the OFF lookup is guarded by `OffJournal` in the controller and only applied when
  `forBarcode == session.barcode`, so a late answer for the previous product cannot hijack the strip.
