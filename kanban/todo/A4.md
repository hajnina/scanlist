## Notes

### Lead note 2026-11-16 (research digest: web-rn-runtime-prior-art.md)
- VisionCamera v5 (2026-04): barcode scanner split out to `react-native-vision-camera-barcode-scanner` (MLKit, MIT, both platforms); frame processors are Nitro Modules; ALWAYS `frame.dispose()`.
- `react-native-vision-camera-mlkit` (MIT) covers OCR + barcode in one plugin (RN 0.86+, VisionCamera ≥5). `frameProcessInterval` is deprecated and has NO throttle effect in v2 — implement your own ~2.5 fps gate.
- Frame→JPEG has no first-party API: routes are Skia frame processor `encodeToBase64` (interop reportedly fixed in v5) or `Photo.getFileDataAsync()` from a `CameraPhotoOutput`. Cheaper alternative: `useResizer` (GPU resizer, MIT, Android 8.0+, input `pixelFormat:'yuv'`) to ship raw RGB and let the server resize/normalise — no JPEG encoder on device.
