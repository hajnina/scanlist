### Lead note 2026-11-16 (vec contract — from the S2 adversarial review, now fixed on main)
- `flatten()` output carries **no** `vec` key (FLATTEN.md §1 exact); `vec` belongs on the **input items** passed to `rank()` (RANK.md §1: `vec: float[256]|null`); rank `entries` also carry no `vec`. When serializing golden.json, do NOT add `vec` to flattened arrays — attach vectors separately and store per-item vectors as their own field, so TS/Kotlin flatteners (§1-exact) match byte-for-byte.
- The S2 ranker on main is the oracle: `shared/ref/scanlist_ref.py` + 74 pytest cases (numpy==2.5.5, pytest==9.2.0 pinned in shared/ref/requirements.txt — reuse the same venv spec). Its `rank()` is deterministic (sha1-stable across processes); differential-tested 4000/4000 vs RANK.md §9 by the reviewer.

### Lead note 2026-11-16 (research digest: camera-to-list-prior-art.md)
- No public project re-ranks a list by camera similarity — this is the missing prior art. Closest designed pipeline: `ticiive/Smart-Fridge-User-Presented-Item-Recognition` (NO licence — study patterns, copy nothing): one reference image per product, OCR keyword index keeping only words unique to one product, CLIP with accept ≥0.66 AND top1/top2 margin ≥0.05, reject <0.55, else defer to a VLM; EMA α=0.50 (matches ours), one decision per pass, sharpest-of-6-crops, brightness gate, 11-frame pass timeout / 60-frame cap.
- 2026 Visual Product Search Benchmark (arXiv 2603.17186): general embeddings get only ~35–44 R@1 on instance-level image→image retrieval (SigLIP2 43.4, Gemini Embedding 43.8, DINOv3 41.9; domain-trained 58.3); EmbeddingGemma 2 unbenchmarked → the image bar is measured, not assumed. The missing primitive vs a plain threshold is the MARGIN rule — propose it in PREFIX_AB.md.
